# Anouchka

Dr. Anouchka Laurent earned her Ph.D. in Oncogenesis from Gustave Roussy
Institute and University Paris Diderot – Paris VII in France where she studied
the genetic and molecular mechanisms of leukemia and lymphoma associated with
gains of chromosome 21. She is currently an Associate Research Scientist in
the Palomero Laboratory at Columbia University, where she investigates the
mechanisms of transformation in peripheral T-cell lymphoma, with a particular
focus on the role of the oncogenic regulator ID2 and its impact on chromatin
regulation and tumor biology.
