# Laura

Dr. Laura Quevedo earned her Ph.D. in Molecular Biology and Biomedicine from
the University of Cantabria under the mentorship of Dr. Ignacio Varela, where
she studied genetic and functional intratumoral heterogeneity in pancreatic
cancer. She is currently a postdoctoral fellow in the Palomero Laboratory at
Columbia University, where she investigates the role of chromatin regulators
in hematopoietic stem cell biology and T-cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia.
