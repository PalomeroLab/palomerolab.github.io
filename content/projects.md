# Ongoing Projects

## Targeting ID2 in Peripheral T-Cell Lymphoma

_Columbia University · 2025–2026_ Elucidating the epigenetic mechanisms driving
ID2 overexpression in mature T-cell lymphomas, and evaluating small-molecule ID2
inhibition as a therapeutic strategy.

## Microenvironment Determinants in PTCL

_Blood Cancer United · 2024–2027_ Interrogating how genetic drivers and viral
infection shape tumor–microenvironment interactions across major PTCL subtypes,
using single-nucleus profiling of patient samples.

## PHF6 and Hematopoietic Stem Cell Aging

_NIH / NIA · 2021–2027_ Using cellular and animal models to define how PHF6 loss
reverses aging phenotypes in hematopoietic stem and progenitor cells.

## VAV1 Alterations in Peripheral T-Cell Lymphomas

_NIH / NCI · 2021–2026_ Identifying the role of the VAV1–MYO1F fusion in T-cell
development and malignant transformation using a conditional knock-in mouse
model and single-cell approaches.

**On the horizon:** the lab is also developing GET, an interpretable foundation
model for decoding regulatory circuits in PTCL, and investigating S1PR1 as a
therapeutic target in AITL — both with pending NIH and foundation support.
