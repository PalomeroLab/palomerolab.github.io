# Join Us!

**Postdoctoral Fellows** We welcome inquiries from postdoctoral candidates
interested in cancer genomics, epigenetics, mouse models, or single-cell and
computational approaches to T-cell malignancies. Current projects span PHF6
biology, VAV1 signaling, tumor microenvironment analysis, and AI-based
regulatory network modeling.

**Students & Rotations** PhD rotation students, master's students, and
undergraduate researchers are welcome to reach out about joining the lab for a
rotation, thesis project, or research experience alongside our current team.

> **Stable, continuous funding.** The lab's research is supported by active NIH
> R01 and U01 grants (NIH/NCI, NIH/NIA) and foundation awards from Blood Cancer
> United, providing a well-resourced environment to build your own project.
